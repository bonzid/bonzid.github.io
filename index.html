About me

Hi! My name is Doria, I am a PhD student in Computer Science at LORIA, focusing on Natural Language Processing, dialogue systems, and data science. My thesis, supervised by Irina Illina (Université de Lorraine) and Fabrice Lefèvre (Université d'Avignon), aims to develop a socially-aware conversational agent designed to support communication skills training for medical students.


My research explores several key directions, including:

  • LLM-based simulation of patient-doctor dialogues for medical education,
  • persona-driven dialogue generation and the modeling of social, emotional, and behavioral characteristics,
  • evaluation of conversational coherence, factual consistency, and behavioral realism across multi-turn interactions.


More broadly, my work lies at the intersection of conversational AI, human-computer interaction, and medical education. My goal is to contribute to the development of virtual patients that can exhibit diverse and consistent personalities, respond naturally to different communication strategies, and provide realistic opportunities for clinical communication training.
